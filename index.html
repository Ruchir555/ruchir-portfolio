01QUANTUM SIMULATION

Qubit dephasing & dynamical decoupling

A Bloch-vector simulation comparing free evolution, Hahn echo, and CPMG pulse sequences for a single qubit. Includes dephasing-rate sweeps, benchmark plots, and CSV output.

Uses ideal instantaneous pulses with z-axis precession and exponential transverse decay.

Python · NumPyView code ↗
02QUANTUM INFORMATION

Majorization & T-transformations

A research notebook for finding sequences of T-transformations between numerical vectors related by majorization. Reports the index choices and mixing parameters for each transformation.

Developed in the context of state transfer between bosonic modes and qubits.
